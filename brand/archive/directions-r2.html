Why it looks like a bank, and three ways out

What makes round 1 read as "bank":
  • Dark, muted green with gold. Thai banks are colour-coded, and dark green is a bank colour there (Kasikorn). Darker, less saturated colour reads as "competent and serious" (Labrecque & Milne 2012). That's the right feeling for money, the wrong one for learning.
  • Grey-green canvas and lots of white. It's corporate restraint, with no warmth or texture.
  • Flat geometric monoline marks in a rounded square. That's the standard fintech app-icon formula.
  • White text on dark fills. Bright fills with dark text feel more playful.
  • No character. Learning brands nearly always have a person, a creature or a handmade quality.
Kept in every direction: the name, the leaf, the looped card font, AA contrast, ✓ / ✕ on feedback. These only change the mood tokens: canvas, brightness, display font, shapes and a mascot.
